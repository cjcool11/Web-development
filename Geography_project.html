Here's some geography facts to read when bored:

Mount Everest

Mount Everest is the tallest mountain above sea level, standing at approximately 8,849 meters (29,032 feet).

The Pacific Ocean

The Pacific Ocean is the largest and deepest ocean on Earth, covering more than 30% of the planet's surface.

The Sahara Desert

The Sahara Desert is the largest hot desert in the world and stretches across much of North Africa.

The Amazon River
The Amazon River carries more water than any other river on Earth and flows through several South American countries.
Greenland

Despite its name, Greenland is mostly covered by a massive ice sheet.

Countries and Continents

Asia is the largest continent by both population and land area.

Russia

Russia is the world's largest country and spans across both Europe and Asia.

Australia

Australia is unique because it is both a country and a continent.

Africa
Africa is home to 54 recognized countries and contains the world's longest river, the Nile.
Antarctica

Antarctica is the coldest continent and contains about 70% of Earth's fresh water in the form of ice.

Interesting Landforms

The Grand Canyon was carved primarily by the Colorado River over millions of years.

Volcanoes

Many volcanoes are found along the Ring of Fire, a region surrounding much of the Pacific Ocean.

Islands

Indonesia consists of more than 17,000 islands, making it one of the largest archipelagos in the world.

Valleys
Valleys are often formed by rivers or glaciers slowly shaping the landscape over thousands of years.
Waterfalls

Angel Falls in Venezuela is the world's tallest uninterrupted waterfall.

Earth's Features

About 71% of the Earth's surface is covered by water.

Time Zones

There are multiple time zones around the world because Earth rotates on its axis.

The Equator

The Equator divides Earth into the Northern and Southern Hemispheres.

The North Pole
The North Pole is located in the Arctic Ocean and is covered by shifting sea ice.
The South Pole

The South Pole sits on the continent of Antarctica at the southernmost point on Earth.
